UNDERSTAND THE MATH

Simple assumptions. Clear decisions.

01

Set your target

Annual retirement expenses divided by your chosen withdrawal rate gives an initial target in today's money.

02

Account for inflation

Your future target grows with inflation. The same lifestyle generally costs more in future rupees.

03

Stress-test the plan

Try lower returns, higher inflation, and a lower withdrawal rate. Markets do not deliver smooth returns every year.

Important methodology note

This tool uses a constant-return model for illustration. Real portfolios have volatility, taxes, fees, sequence-of-returns risk, and changing spending. Education goals are shown separately and are not deducted from the FIRE target. Get qualified advice before making major financial decisions.
