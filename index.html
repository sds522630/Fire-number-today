UNDERSTAND THE MATH

Detailed inputs. Simple calculation.

01

Add your expenses

Monthly costs stay monthly. Annual costs are divided by 12 so irregular bills are not forgotten.

02

Choose retirement costs

Keep “Retire?” checked for expenses likely to continue. Exclude costs that genuinely disappear before retirement.

03

Calculate the corpus

Annual retirement spending ÷ withdrawal rate gives Core FIRE. Then the app adds your safety buffer and separate goals.

Avoid double counting

If children's education is entered as a separate goal, do not also include the same amount as a retirement expense. Housing, healthcare and family support should be reviewed carefully rather than automatically removed.
